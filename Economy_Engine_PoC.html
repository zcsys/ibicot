Wholesale element markets
Prices move from realized downstream order imbalance. Zero-demand elements have downward price pressure toward their cost floor, while positive unmet demand creates upward pressure. HHI is calculated from realized transaction volume for the current tick: 0.000 means no observed transactions and 1.000 means one supplier captured all realized volume. Manufacturers procure raw inputs for near-term production needs rather than attempting to refill the entire finished-goods target at once; any positive shortfall is rounded up to the minimum wholesale lot. Multi-input manufacturers buy requested inputs directly; there is no additional cash-reserve allocation across inputs.

Demand / fulfillment
Demand is generated continuously from customer-level demand curves, but physical goods are indivisible: every potential, desired, bought, sold, and fulfilled quantity is rounded to a whole unit. Potential demand is the latent quantity at zero price; price-adjusted demand is the whole-unit quantity desired at the best effective supplier price; remaining unmet demand is caused by insufficient stock at the chosen supplier.

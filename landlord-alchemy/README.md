# LandLord Alchemy

An idle/clicker game idea that mixes **land**, **alchemy**, and a **free market**.
The full pitch is the 16-slide deck [`LandLordAlchemyV0.pdf`](LandLordAlchemyV0.pdf).

## The core loop

Buy **Land** → click to earn **Money** → extract the four base elements
(**Air, Water, Fire, Earth**) → combine them via **Alchemy** into higher tiers →
sell the results on an open **Marketplace**.

## The tiers

- **Tier 0 — Land.** Land is the world's carrying capacity. Clicking earns money
  (`1 click = game tier × (1 + log₁₀(Lp))`) while lowering the price of land.
  **Gaia Points** are a limited real-money currency; land pricing scales to keep
  the game from becoming pay-to-win.
- **Tier 1 — Energy & Work Force.** A predator–prey (Lotka–Volterra) loop with
  sliders for energy share, work-force health, and energy quality, plus
  **Science Points**; this is where Air / Water / Fire / Earth are extracted.
- **Tier 2 — Alchemy.** Combine elements (`λ·A + λ·B = λ·C`) into ~4,027
  elements across six tiers. New recipes are teased through social media so the
  community helps "discover" them.
- **Tier 3 — Marketplace.** *The real game*: a free market with banking and
  lending, where players trade products and money.

Also included: a **Vault** (card collection with timed production), a **LAB**
(experiment to discover recipes), and a future hard-copy card game.

Repeats are allowed in both tiers; reversing an ingredient pair does not create a new recipe. Parenthesized structures remain distinct even when raw ancestry is equal. Every output occupies its complexity in storage spaces. Half of goods space is reserved for finished goods; input space is weighted by quantity × item space.

Product IDs come from the full universe and are retained in the subset, so gaps are intentional. Input IDs specify immediate products, not flattened ancestry. Sector membership requires its preferred material to be a direct input.

## Fourteen paired sectors

Each refinery material has one T1 sector and one corresponding T2 application sector. All recipes are assigned. Every T2 recipe directly consumes its sector’s preferred T1 material. Recipes with a single eligible sector are assigned first; those with two eligible sectors go to the less populated eligible sector, breaking ties by sector ID. This replaces round-robin allocation. Sector sizes follow ingredient eligibility and are not forced equal.

## Preserved SDK edge observation

The extra strict five-point full-tile helper initially fails the tablet row's rightmost sample. The tile occupies x0..320, y342..491; the Flutter detail pane's RenderMouseRegion begins at x316 and intercepts x316.8. `font-tablet-hit-diagnostic.json` records the actual hit paths, not an inferred scrollbar explanation. The full label is within x16..296 and remains readable. The final probe measures a conservative **315×149px effective tappable region**, validates five points within that region, confirms named tap semantics, real keyboard focus/Enter and pointer activation, and explicitly records `full_tile_five_point_pass:false` on tablet. Phone still passes the original full-tile five-point helper.

This4px SDK overlap is a known nonblocking behavior against the original large/readable/tappable control requirement: it obscures no label, leaves a substantial target, and does not prevent keyboard, pointer or navigation. It does not satisfy the additional full-tile near-edge invariant, and this report does not claim that it does. The shared strict helper and production SDK were not changed. The lead reviewed the distinction before the final targeted qualification.

# Night of Returning Stars

## upcoming

A new night sky is approaching. Every crew will begin with the same small fleet.

## live

Build a separate fleet and discover how the returning stars change its connections. Your main harbor keeps working.

## ended

The public watch has ended. Your fleet and rewards are safe in the archive.

## archived

Continue your fleet whenever you like. Your selected pass can still earn XP from the same daily contracts.

## pass

Choose one pass for new contract XP. Free rewards and premium appearances use the same 30-level track.

## premium

Premium ownership adds ten appearances. It adds no XP, currency, storage or production. Verified purchase restoration is required.

## alternative

Assign this daily slot to the seasonal fleet. Complete its frozen production target to claim that same slot once.

## archive_resume

Resume your archived fleet from its saved position. Time spent while the archive is paused grants no fleet production.

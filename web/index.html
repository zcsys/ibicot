Consumers

Demand & fulfillment

—

Star Business is a human–robot manufacturing colony building itself into the supply base for a coming galactic war. Its output covers housing, medical equipment, gardens, archives, rescue vehicles, and everyday trade. One million consumers buy, switch suppliers, and keep the colony running; prices, reliability, loyalty, supply, and demand emerge from their decisions. They purchase only C-3–C-5 manufactured goods; refined materials supply manufacturers exclusively. Every market delivers a physical good. The consumer count stays fixed, and final purchasing budgets are unlimited in this model.

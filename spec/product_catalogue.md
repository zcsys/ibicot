# Midbridge product map

Midbridge is an Earth colony running a robot-led consumer behavior experiment with exactly 1,000,000 immortal human consumers. Residents are selected individuals who sought immortality and worked their way toward sealing the Infinity contract. Robots provide for their needs and receive their consumerism in return. Most humans elsewhere work in many roles for robots under poor conditions. Immortality Treatment is an ordinary healthcare purchase; colony population remains fixed, with no additional health or demographic dynamics.

Tier 0 buys resources from Gaia through extraction and sells to companies. Tier 1 buys and sells only to companies. Tier 2 buys Tier 1 materials and sells finished goods to humans. Public demand for all Tier 1 materials is zero.

## Sixty distinct Tier 2 products

Each of the ten sectors contains six products: two C-3, two C-4 and two C-5. Every product has one distinct name and code, a unique unordered Tier 1 recipe, and a unique expanded elemental composition. Codes run from T2-001 to T2-060.

Recipes produce one unit and show quantities explicitly. Ingredient order does not change identity: WA + F and F + WA describe the same Yoga Pants product. Products with the same expanded elemental composition are also excluded. These are abstract game recipes.

In the **Tier 1 recipe** column, W/E/F/A and WE/WF/WA/EF/EA/FA refer to the ten processed materials above; compound codes are abbreviated without their internal plus sign. In the **Elemental composition** column, W/E/F/A mean raw Water/Earth/Fire/Air ancestry. Complexity is the total number of elemental units.
